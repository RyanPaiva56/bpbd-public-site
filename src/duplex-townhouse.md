- Duplexes and town houses take advantage of real estate property that is zoned.
- They allow for two residences to be on one piece of property.
- Quite often they are less expensive than building two separate single-family residences.
- Duplexes and town houses have been used by investors to live in one half and rent out the other side to pay for the mortgage.
