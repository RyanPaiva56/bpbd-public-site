Detached garages allow for a structure to be away from the main house.

- They can distance themselves away from any noisy activity such as auto mechanics, woodworking, lawn equipment maintenance.
- Detached garages give you the ability to store flammable liquids and propane tanks.
- Detached garages could be converted into living space at a later time. Allowing for an in law suite separate from the main home.
- Detached garages allow protection for additional automobiles, boats, lawn equipment.
- They have advantages over attached additions to existing home, because there is no concern for the way it would be attached and maybe take away from the look of the existing home.
