Blueprints By Design wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

## Our goal

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), Level AA. We test the site with automated accessibility tools and by navigating it with a keyboard.

## Known limitations

- **Videos** are hosted on YouTube. We work to make sure each one has accurate captions. The YouTube player itself is provided by YouTube.
- **The map** on our [contact page](/contact/) is provided by Google Maps. Our address is also listed as text on that page.
- **The scanned survey forms PDF** on our [reviews page](/reviews/) contains images of handwritten forms. Their text is included on the reviews page itself.
