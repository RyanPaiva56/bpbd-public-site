- Pool houses and guesthouse allow the pool area access to a detached building.
- As a pool house it could provide restroom, shower facilities, a dressing room, game room, and a covered front porch.
- If used as a game room it would keep the noise away from the main house.
- Guest houses provide a living space that is a detached structure that could be used for family, friends, or in-laws.
- Volusia County property appraiser’s office has in the past adjusted the property tax if it was for family in-laws and they would not assess the full value of the addition to the property.
- Inquiring to your local property appraiser’s office to determine the family in law valuation.
- Pool houses and guesthouse allow for privacy separate from the main house.
